# Brag Plan: JARVIS Deck — Remake (Nov 13, 2026)

## Why a remake
The Nov 12 video predates a big iteration: the grid was repacked (no dead space,
bigger text), cards gained click-through actions, JARVIS answers clicks with
contextual rotating spoken lines (subject-cased), the voice card is
business-line-first, automation shows in-place fix status, and the deck has five
live tabs. D asked for: updated layout · updated features · JARVIS's multiple
responses · all tabs showcased.

## What is this app?
Unchanged: open-source, local-first control deck for a personal AI agent — plasma
neural orb, memory galaxy, live services, voice console.

## The angle
Same fantasy, now with proof of growth: this thing ALIVE — it answers you
differently every time, heals itself, and runs your whole business from five tabs.

## Hook (first 2–3s)
Unchanged (it works): orb ignition + "YOUR AGENT HAS A COCKPIT NOW."

2. **All five tabs** — beat-locked cuts: Vision · Money · Goals · Settings
